PNG to JPG converter

Convert PNG images to smaller JPG files. Transparent areas become white.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

JPG files are much smaller for photos, and every website and form accepts them.

How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Choose one or more PNG files.
  2. Set the quality: 85–92% looks the same as the original for photos.
  3. Press Convert to JPG, then download each file or all of them as one ZIP.

PNG or JPG: which should you use?

  • JPG is best for photos. Files are much smaller, but there is no transparency.
  • PNG is best for screenshots, logos, text and graphics with sharp edges or transparent parts. It is lossless, so files are larger.

A photo saved as PNG is often 3–10 times larger than the same photo as JPG, so converting is the quickest way to get under an upload limit. To shrink it even more, use the Image Compressor.

Questions

What happens to transparent areas?

They become white, because JPG can't store transparency. Keep the PNG if you need a see-through background.

Will I lose quality?

JPG is lossy, but at 90% quality the difference is invisible in photos. Screenshots with small text may look slightly softer; keep those as PNG.
