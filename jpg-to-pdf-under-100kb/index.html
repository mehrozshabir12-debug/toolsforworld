JPG to PDF under 100KB

Turn photos or phone scans into a single PDF that is just under 100 KB, ready for admission and exam forms with a strict 100 KB limit.

How to make a PDF under 100 KB from photos

  1. Choose your JPG, PNG or WebP photos. Use the arrows to put the pages in order.
  2. The limit is already set to 100 KB. Change it if your form allows a different size.
  3. Press Create PDF and download the file.

How it works

The PDF is first made at full quality. If it is bigger than 100 KB, every photo is re-saved a little smaller and the tool searches for the sharpest level at which the whole PDF still fits. Nothing is uploaded: your photos are processed by your own browser.

How many pages fit in 100 KB?

At 100 KB, one or two pages work best. A single page of a typed document stays readable; a full-colour photo is reduced to around 1000 px on its longest side. Fewer pages always means sharper pages.

Tips for a clear result

  • Crop each photo to the page edges first. A table or floor around the page wastes space.
  • Take scans in daylight, straight from above, so the text is dark on a white page.
  • Already have a PDF that is too big? Use Compress PDF to 100KB instead.

Questions

Is this the same as “image to PDF under 100 KB” or “photo to PDF less than 100 KB”?

Yes. JPG to PDF, image to PDF and photo to PDF all mean the same here, and under, below, less than and up to 100 KB all mean the PDF must be 100 KB or smaller.

What if the PDF can't get under 100 KB?

The tool shows the smallest size it could reach. Remove a page, crop the photos tighter or use a higher limit if your form allows it.

Can I do this on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone's browser, choose photos from your gallery and download the PDF. No app is needed.
