HEIC to PDF converter

Turn iPhone photos (.heic) into a single PDF in one step: no need to convert to JPG first. Reorder the pages, pick a page size, and nothing is uploaded.

How to convert HEIC to PDF

  1. Choose one or more .heic photos. You can mix in JPG and PNG files too.
  2. Put them in order with the ▲ ▼ arrows.
  3. Pick a page size (A4, Letter or Same as image) and a margin.
  4. Press Create PDF and download the file.

Why iPhone photos need converting

Since iOS 11, iPhones save photos as HEIC by default, because the files are about half the size of JPG. Many upload portals, Windows PCs and printers can't open HEIC, and most forms want documents as PDF anyway. This tool turns each HEIC photo into a high-quality JPG inside the PDF, all on your device.

Great for

  • Documents photographed on an iPhone: ID cards, certificates, receipts and signed forms for job, visa or university applications.
  • Homework and notes: several pages photographed in class, submitted as one PDF.
  • Claims and records: photos of damage or purchases for insurance or expense reports.

Keeping the PDF small

Phone photos are large. If the portal has a limit, type it in Max PDF size (for example 2048 for 2 MB) and the photos are made just small enough to fit. Or shrink a finished PDF with Compress PDF.

Want separate JPG files instead? Use HEIC to JPG. Tip: on iPhone, Settings → Camera → Formats → "Most Compatible" makes new photos JPG from the start.

Questions

Is HEIC to PDF free?

Yes. There is no limit, sign-up or watermark.

Can I put several HEIC photos into one PDF?

Yes. Choose them all, reorder them with the arrows, and each photo becomes one page of the same PDF.

Are my iPhone photos uploaded?

No. They are converted and put into the PDF inside your browser.

Can I mix HEIC with JPG or PNG?

Yes. HEIC photos are converted and JPG or PNG files are added as they are.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open this page in Safari, choose photos from your library or the Files app, and download the PDF.
