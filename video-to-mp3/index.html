Video to MP3 converter

Extract the sound from a video file and save it as MP3: MOV from an iPhone, WebM from a screen recorder, MKV, MP4 and more. The file stays on your device.

How to convert a video to MP3

  1. Choose a video from your computer or phone.
  2. Pick the quality: 128 kbps for talks and lectures, 192 kbps for most uses, 320 kbps for music.
  3. Press Convert to MP3 and download the audio.

Which files work?

Any video or audio file that your browser can play: MP4, MOV (iPhone and Mac), M4V, WebM (most screen recorders and browser recordings), 3GP (older Android phones) and many MKV files in Chrome and Edge. Audio files such as WAV, M4A, AAC and OGG can be turned into MP3 too. If a file won't convert in one browser, try Chrome on a computer.

What people use it for

  • Lectures and meetings: keep only the audio of a recorded class or Zoom call, to listen to on the go or send for transcription.
  • Interviews and podcasts: pull the sound from a camera or phone recording to edit it in an audio app.
  • Your own music and voice-overs: save the soundtrack of a video you made.
  • Smaller files: one hour of speech at 128 kbps is about 58 MB, much smaller than the video.

Good to know

  • The MP3 has the same length as the video. Mono videos stay mono; stereo stays stereo.
  • Very long videos (over about an hour) need a lot of memory and work best on a computer.
  • A video with no sound track can't give an MP3.
Only convert videos you made yourself or have permission to use. Downloading from YouTube or other streaming sites is not supported.

Questions

Is video to MP3 free?

Yes. There is no limit, sign-up or watermark.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The audio is extracted and encoded inside your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

Can I convert MOV or WebM to MP3?

Yes. MOV, WebM, MP4 and M4V work in modern browsers, and many MKV files work in Chrome and Edge. If one browser can't read a file, try Chrome on a computer.

Which bitrate should I choose?

128 kbps is fine for speech, 192 kbps suits most audio, and 320 kbps is the best MP3 quality for music.

Can I convert a YouTube link?

No. The tool only converts files you already have on your device, such as your own recordings.
