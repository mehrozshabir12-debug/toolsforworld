Wi-Fi QR code generator

Create a QR code that connects phones to your Wi-Fi in one scan. Replace the network name and password in the box.

How to make a Wi-Fi QR code

  1. In the box, replace MyNetwork with your Wi-Fi name and MyPassword with the password.
  2. Keep T:WPA for most routers (use T:WEP for very old ones, or T:nopass for open networks).
  3. Download the PNG and print it for guests.

iPhone and Android cameras both read Wi-Fi QR codes and offer to join the network.

Questions

Is my Wi-Fi password sent anywhere?

No. The QR code is made in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
