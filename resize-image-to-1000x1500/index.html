Resize image to 1000×1500 pixels

Get a photo that is exactly 1000 × 1500 px for Pinterest pins in the recommended 2:3 shape. Choose crop to fill, or fit with a white border.

How to resize to 1000×1500

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. The size is already set to 1000 × 1500.
  3. Pick Crop to fill (no empty space) or Fit with white border (nothing cut off), then press Resize.

Why exact size matters

Platforms crop or blur images that are the wrong shape. Uploading at exactly 1000 × 1500 px keeps your photo sharp and framed the way you want.

Questions

Will my photo be stretched?

No. Crop to fill keeps the proportions and trims the edges; Fit with white border keeps the whole photo and adds a border.
