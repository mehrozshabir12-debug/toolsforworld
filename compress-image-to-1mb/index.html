Compress image to 1MB

Choose a photo and it is made just under 1 MB automatically, keeping as much quality as possible. Ideal for photos that must stay under 1 MB.

How to compress a photo to 1MB

  1. Choose your photo (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. The target is already set to 1 MB. Change it if you need a different size.
  3. Press Compress and download the photo.

How it works

The tool tries many quality levels and picks the highest one that stays under 1 MB. If the photo is still too big at the lowest quality, it is scaled down a little at a time until it fits. Everything happens on your device, so your photo is never uploaded.

Tips

  • Crop away empty background first: less detail means better quality at 1 MB.
  • Most forms want JPG. Keep the output on JPG unless WebP is allowed.

How big is a 1 MB photo?

For a typical phone photo saved as JPG, 1 MB fits roughly about 2000 × 2700 pixels at good quality. Simple images (a signature, a face on a plain wall) fit more pixels; busy scenes with lots of detail fit fewer.

A 1 MB photo is close to print quality for small prints, and it still fits email and most forms with a 1 MB limit.

Where 1 MB limits appear

Many forms, CMS uploads and email systems set a 1 MB limit per image. A phone photo is often 3–5 MB, so it needs compressing, but at 1 MB it still looks nearly identical to the original on any screen.

Questions

Is this the same as “resize image to 1 MB”?

Yes. When a form asks you to resize a photo to 1 MB, it means the file size. This tool lowers the file size to 1 MB and only reduces the width and height if it has to.

Will my photo still look good at 1MB?

For most photos, yes. The tool keeps the best quality that fits. Very large, detailed photos may be scaled down to fit.

Can I compress several photos to 1MB at once?

Yes. Choose several files and each one is compressed to the target size.

Is 1 MB the same as 1000 KB?

Not exactly. Most upload forms treat 1 MB as 1024 KB. This tool uses 1024 KB, so the result passes either way.

How do I check my photo's size in KB?

On Windows, right-click the photo and choose Properties. On a Mac, select it and press Cmd + I. On Android or iPhone, open the photo and look at its details or info. The tool also shows the new size after compressing.
