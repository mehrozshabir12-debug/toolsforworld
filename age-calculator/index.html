Age calculator

Enter a date of birth to get the exact age in years, months and days, and the countdown to the next birthday.

How age is calculated

Full years are counted first, then whole months, then the remaining days. That's the method used for forms and official documents. Total days include leap days.

Common uses

  • Exam and job forms: many notices give an age limit “as on” a cutoff date. Put that date in “Age on this date”.
  • School admission: check a child's age on the admission date.
  • Retirement and pensions: see exactly when an age is reached.

Questions

How do I find my age on a past or future date?

Change the “Age on this date” field to any date.

How is age calculated for someone born on 29 February?

In years without 29 February, the birthday is counted on 1 March.

Can I find my age on an exam's cutoff date?

Yes. Set “Age on this date” to the cutoff date given in the notice.
