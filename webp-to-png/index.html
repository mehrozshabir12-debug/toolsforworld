WebP to PNG converter

Convert WebP images to PNG so they open in any app, with transparent backgrounds kept. Convert many files at once, without uploading them.

How to convert WebP to PNG

  1. Choose one or more .webp files.
  2. Press Convert to PNG.
  3. Download each PNG, or all of them as one ZIP.

WebP to PNG or WebP to JPG?

Pick PNG when the image has a transparent background (logos, stickers, icons, product cut-outs), or when it is a graphic with text, flat colours and sharp edges. PNG keeps the transparency and does not add JPG blur. Pick WebP to JPG for ordinary photos: the files are much smaller and transparent areas become white.

Why WebP files cause trouble

Websites use WebP because it makes pages load faster. When you save an image from a browser, it often arrives as .webp. Many older photo editors, some versions of Microsoft Office, printers, marketplaces and upload forms still don't accept it, while PNG opens everywhere.

Good to know

  • The PNG has the same width and height as the WebP; nothing is cropped or resized.
  • Converting can't add detail that the WebP never had, so a blurry WebP stays blurry.
  • PNG files are usually bigger than the WebP. Need them smaller for an upload? Use the Image Compressor.
  • Animated WebP: only the first frame is saved, because a PNG holds a single image.

Questions

Does WebP to PNG keep transparency?

Yes. Transparent areas in the WebP stay transparent in the PNG.

Is the conversion lossless?

Saving as PNG adds no extra loss. The PNG shows exactly what the WebP shows.

Can I convert many WebP files at once?

Yes. Choose several files and download them one by one or as a ZIP.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser, on your device.

Why is the PNG bigger than the WebP?

WebP compresses very efficiently, and PNG stores every pixel exactly, so the PNG is usually larger. That is normal.
