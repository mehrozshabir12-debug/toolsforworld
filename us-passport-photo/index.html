US passport photo maker

Crop your photo to the US size of 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) and download a single photo plus a 4×6 inch sheet to print.

How to make a US passport photo

  1. Take a photo facing the camera against a plain white or off-white background, with even light and no shadows.
  2. Choose the photo. The size is already set to 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm).
  3. Use zoom and the sliders so the top of the head is near the upper guide line and the chin near the lower one.
  4. Press Make sheet. Print the 4×6 sheet at 100% scale at home or at a photo shop, or use the single photo for online applications.

Photo checklist

  • Size: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm).
  • Background: plain white or off-white, with no patterns or objects.
  • Neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open and visible.
  • No glasses glare, hats or filters.

US passport and visa photo rules at a glance

  • Size 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm); head 1 to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of hair.
  • Plain white or off-white background, taken in the last 6 months.
  • No glasses: the US has not accepted glasses in passport photos since November 2016.
  • US visa (DS-160) digital photo: a square JPEG between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels and no larger than 240 KB. The single photo from this tool is 600 × 600 px.

Official rules: travel.state.gov passport photos.

Rules change from time to time. Always check the latest photo requirements on the official government website before you apply.

Questions

What size is a US passport photo?

2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). This tool crops your photo to exactly that size at 300 dpi.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping and the print sheet are made in your browser, so the photo stays on your device.

Can I use this photo for a US visa (DS-160)?

Yes, the single photo is a 600 × 600 px JPEG, usually well under 240 KB. Check it in the State Department's photo tool before submitting.
