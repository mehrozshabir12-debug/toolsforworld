Why Tools for World

Your files stay with you

PDFs, photos and scans are processed inside your browser. They are never sent to a server, so a passport scan or a payslip never leaves your device. Check it yourself in one minute.

Made for real forms

Job portals, visa forms and universities ask for exact sizes: a photo under 50 KB, a PDF under 2 MB, a 35 × 45 mm passport photo. Each of these has its own ready-set page.

Not sure where to start?

Most visitors arrive with a deadline: an application closes tonight and the portal keeps saying the file is too big or in the wrong format. Start with the error message. "File exceeds limit" means compress it; "invalid format" means convert it; "photo dimensions incorrect" means resize or crop it. If a scan is unreadable after shrinking, rescan in daylight rather than compressing harder, and if you have several pages, put them into one PDF before you upload.
