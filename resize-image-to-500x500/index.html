Resize image to 500×500 pixels

Get a photo that is exactly 500 × 500 px for WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal profile pictures. Choose crop to fill, or fit with a white border. Need a different size, like 3000 × 400 or 700 × 500? Just type your own width and height in the boxes.

How to resize to 500×500

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. The size is already set to 500 × 500.
  3. Pick Crop to fill (no empty space) or Fit with white border (nothing cut off), then press Resize.

Why exact size matters

Platforms crop or blur images that are the wrong shape. Uploading at exactly 500 × 500 px keeps your photo sharp and framed the way you want.

Profile pictures for WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal

Chat apps show your photo as a small circle and compress it heavily. 500 × 500 px is plenty and uploads instantly on slow connections.

  • Circle crop: keep your face in the centre; the corners are never seen.
  • Group icons work the same way. A logo or simple symbol is easier to recognise than a busy photo.
  • Business accounts: use your logo on a plain background so customers recognise you in their chat list.

WhatsApp DP size

WhatsApp shows your profile photo as a circle cut from a square, so any square image works. 500 × 500 px is a safe size: sharp on every phone and small enough to upload quickly. If your photo is a rectangle, WhatsApp asks you to crop it; to keep the whole picture instead, choose Fit with white border here first. Keep your face in the middle, because the corners are hidden by the circle.

Questions

Will my photo be stretched?

No. Crop to fill keeps the proportions and trims the edges; Fit with white border keeps the whole photo and adds a border.

Can I resize to a size other than 500×500?

Yes. Type any width and height in pixels (up to 20,000) in the size boxes, for example 3000 × 400, 1500 × 400 or 600 × 750, and press Resize.

Why does WhatsApp cut my DP?

WhatsApp needs a square and crops anything else. Resize to 500×500 with Fit with white border to keep the whole picture.

What is the WhatsApp DP size?

Any square photo works because WhatsApp crops it to a circle. 500 × 500 px is a good, safe size.
