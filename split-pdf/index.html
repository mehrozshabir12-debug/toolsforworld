Split PDF

Extract the pages you need, like 1-3, 5, or split every page into its own PDF.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Type the pages to keep, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10, or choose “every page”.
  3. Press Split PDF.

Questions

Does splitting lower the quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are.
