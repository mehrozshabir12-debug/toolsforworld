Split PDF

Extract the pages you need, like 1-3, 5, or split every page into its own PDF.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Type the pages to keep, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10, or choose “every page”.
  3. Press Split PDF.

Examples

  • 1-3: pages 1, 2 and 3 in one new PDF.
  • 1, 4, 7: just those three pages.
  • 2-10, 12: drop page 1 and page 11.
  • Every page as a separate PDF: one file per page, which you can download together as a ZIP.

Pages are copied exactly, so text stays selectable and quality doesn't change.

What you can do with it

  • Extract a few pages: send only the signed page of a contract, or one chapter of a textbook you own.
  • Remove pages: list every page except the ones you don't want, for example 1-4, 6-12 to drop page 5.
  • Split every page: turn a scanned stack of forms into separate files, one per page.
  • Reorder: list pages in a new order, like 3, 1, 2.

Writing page ranges

Separate items with commas and use a dash for ranges. 2-5 means pages 2, 3, 4 and 5. Spaces are fine. Page numbers are the real position in the file, which can differ from the numbers printed on the pages if the document has a cover or roman-numbered intro.

Want to combine the pieces with other files later? Use Merge PDF.

Questions

Does splitting lower the quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are.

How do I delete pages from a PDF?

Type the pages you want to keep. For example, to remove page 5 from a 10-page PDF, type 1-4, 6-10.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

Remove the password first in a PDF reader, then split the unprotected copy.

Is the original file changed?

No. Your original PDF stays as it is; you download new files.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

Remove the password first by opening it in a PDF reader and saving a copy without protection.
