## Long description

> Tools for World is a free collection of everyday file tools that run entirely in your browser. Compress a PDF or photo to an exact size like 100 KB or 1 MB, resize images to exact pixel sizes for Instagram, YouTube or passport uploads, convert PDF to Word and Word to PDF, merge and split PDFs, turn HEIC and WebP into JPG, remove photo backgrounds, and copy text from images in 14 languages with OCR.
>
> Because the processing happens on your own device, your files are never uploaded to a server. There is no account, no daily limit and no watermark. The site also has passport and visa photo makers for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, India, Schengen and China, plus simple utilities like a word counter, QR code generator and password generator.

## Honest expectations

Most directory links are nofollow, so they don't move Google rankings directly. They help by sending real visitors and making people search for "Tools for World" by name, which Google does notice. The biggest ranking gains still come from the pages themselves (see GROWTH.md).
