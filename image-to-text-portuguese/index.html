Portuguese image to text (OCR)

Extract Portuguese text from a photo or screenshot so you can copy, edit and search it. Works for printed Portuguese text from documents and screenshots.

How to convert Portuguese image to text

  1. Choose a clear photo or screenshot.
  2. The language is already set to Portuguese.
  3. Press Extract text and copy the result.

Tips for accurate Portuguese OCR

  • Use a sharp image with dark text on a light background.
  • Crop to the text only, and keep lines straight.
  • Printed text works much better than handwriting.

About Portuguese text recognition

The Portuguese model reads ã, õ, ç and the other accented vowels, for both Brazilian and European Portuguese printed text.

What people use Portuguese OCR for

Used for documents from Brazil and Portugal: bills, contracts, school material and screenshots of messages.

After extracting, paste the text into Word or Google Docs to fix any mistakes. For multi-page bills and contracts, turn each page into an image with PDF to JPG first, then extract them one by one.

Questions

Is Portuguese OCR free?

Yes, with no limits. The text recognition runs on your own device.

Why are some words wrong?

Blurry photos, unusual fonts and handwriting reduce accuracy. Try a clearer, cropped image.

Does it work for Brazilian Portuguese?

Yes. Both Brazilian and European spelling are printed with the same letters.

Can I extract Portuguese text from a PDF?

Yes. Convert the PDF pages to images with PDF to JPG, then run each image through Portuguese OCR. If the PDF already has selectable text, PDF to Word is faster.
