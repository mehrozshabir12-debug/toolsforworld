HEIC to JPG converter

Convert iPhone photos (.heic / .heif) to JPG so they open on Windows, Android and any website.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose one or more .heic photos.
  2. Set the quality.
  3. Press Convert to JPG.

Tip: on iPhone, Settings → Camera → Formats → “Most Compatible” saves new photos as JPG.

HEIC to PDF

Convert the photos to JPG here first, then put them into one PDF with JPG to PDF. Need the photos smaller for an upload? Run them through the Image Compressor.

Why iPhones save HEIC photos

Since iOS 11, iPhones save photos as HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) by default. HEIC files are about half the size of JPG at similar quality, but many Windows PCs, Android phones, websites and upload forms can't open them. Converting to JPG makes them work everywhere.

Converting many photos? Choose them all at once and download the results together as a ZIP.

Questions

Why can't I open HEIC files?

HEIC is Apple's photo format. Many Windows PCs, Android phones and websites don't support it.

Is JPEG the same as JPG?

Yes. JPG and JPEG are two spellings of the same format; the files are identical.

Will I lose quality?

At 92% quality the JPG looks the same as the HEIC. The JPG file is usually bigger.

Does it work on Windows and Android?

Yes, in any modern browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
