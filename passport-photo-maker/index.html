Tips for a good passport photo

  • Face the camera, plain light background, no shadows.
  • Use the zoom slider so the head fills most of the frame; the guide lines show where the chin and top of head should be.
  • Print the 4×6 sheet at a photo shop or at home at 100% scale.

Always check the latest rules of the office you are applying to.

Common photo sizes

  • 35 × 45 mm: UK, Schengen countries, Australia, India, Pakistan and many others.
  • 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm): US passports and visas.
  • 33 × 48 mm: China visa.

See every country's rules in our passport photo size guide, or open a ready preset: US, UK, India, Australia, Canada, Schengen visa.

Why photos get rejected

  • Shadows on the face or background, or a background that isn't plain.
  • Head too small or too large in the frame.
  • Glasses with glare, or a smile showing teeth where a neutral face is required.
  • Printing at the wrong scale. Always print at 100% / actual size, never “fit to page”.

Questions

How is a passport size photo measured?

Most countries use 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm, about 1.4 × 1.8 inches), measured on the printed photo. The US uses 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). At 300 dpi, 35 × 45 mm is about 413 × 531 pixels.

Can I use this photo for an online application?

Yes. Many online applications want a digital photo instead of a print; download the single photo and check their pixel and KB limits.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping and the print sheet are made in your browser.
