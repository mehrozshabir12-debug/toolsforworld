Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
  • % change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100
  • Add X% = Y × (1 + X ÷ 100) · Take X% off = Y × (1 − X ÷ 100)

Common percentage questions

  • What is 15% of 80? Multiply: 80 × 15 ÷ 100 = 12.
  • 20 is what percent of 80? Divide: 20 ÷ 80 × 100 = 25%.
  • Change from 50 to 65? (65 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 30% increase.

For shopping prices, the discount calculator shows the final price and how much you save.

Everyday examples

  • Exam marks: 42 out of 60 is 42 ÷ 60 × 100 = 70%.
  • Pay rise: from 2,500 to 2,750 is a 10% increase.
  • Tax: adding 10% GST to 150 gives 165; adding 18% to 1,000 gives 1,180.
  • Sale: 25% off 1,000 is 750. See also the Discount Calculator.

Percent vs. percentage points

If an interest rate rises from 4% to 5%, it went up by 1 percentage point, but by 25% in relative terms. News reports mix these up often, so check which one is meant.

A rise and a fall of the same percent don't cancel out: 100 up 20% is 120, and 120 down 20% is 96.

Questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Use “Take X% off”: 25% off 1000 is 750.

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Multiply by 1 plus the percentage. For example, 200 plus 10% = 200 × 1.10 = 220.

How do I find the original price before a discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount ÷ 100). For 80 after 20% off: 80 ÷ 0.8 = 100.

How do I remove tax from a price?

Divide by (1 + tax ÷ 100). For 110 including 10% tax: 110 ÷ 1.1 = 100.
