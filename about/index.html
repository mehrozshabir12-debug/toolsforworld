About Tools for World

Tools for World is a free collection of everyday online tools: PDF and Word converters, image compressors and converters, a background remover, OCR in many languages, passport photo makers, a QR code generator, a password generator and simple calculators.

Why we built it

Most people meet these tasks at awkward moments: a job portal wants a photo under 50 KB, a visa form wants a PDF under 300 KB, or a scanned letter needs to become editable text. Many sites that do this ask you to sign up, add watermarks, or upload private documents to unknown servers. Tools for World was built to do these jobs quickly, for free, without any of that.

Who makes it

Tools for World is an independent project built and maintained by Mehroz Shabir. Every tool is designed, written and tested by hand, and new tools are added based on what visitors ask for.

How the tools work

All tools run inside your browser. Files you open are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. Where a tool needs extra code, such as the OCR language data or the background-removal model, your browser downloads that code, and then the work happens locally. This is why the tools keep working on slow connections once a page has loaded, and why your documents stay private.

What we promise

  • Free: no sign-up, no limits on use and no watermarks.
  • Private: your files stay on your device. See our privacy policy.
  • Accurate guidance: pages about official sizes, such as passport photos, link to the rules and remind you to check the latest requirements with the official authority.
  • Tested: every tool is checked automatically in a real browser whenever the site is updated.

How the site is funded

The site is kept free by advertising. Ads never change how a tool works, and they never have access to your files.
