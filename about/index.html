About Tools for World

Tools for World is a free collection of everyday online tools: PDF and Word converters, image compressors and converters, a background remover, OCR, a QR code generator, a password generator and more.

All tools run inside your browser. Files you open are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers.

We keep the tools simple, fast and free, with no sign-up and no watermarks.

You may also see us written as ToolsForWorld, Tools4World or Tools 4 World. It's the same site: toolsforworld.com.
