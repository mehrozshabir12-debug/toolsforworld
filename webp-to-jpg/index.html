WebP to JPG converter

Convert WebP images, often saved from websites, to JPG so they open everywhere.

What is WebP?

WebP is a modern image format used by many websites. Some apps and forms do not accept it, so converting to JPG is the easy fix.

How to convert WebP to JPG

  1. Choose one or more .webp images.
  2. Pick the quality (92% is a good default).
  3. Press Convert to JPG and download.

Where WebP files come from

Many websites serve images as WebP because the files are 25–35% smaller than JPG at similar quality. When you save an image from a website, you often get a .webp file that older apps, photo editors and upload forms won't open. JPG works everywhere.

Why you end up with WebP files

Many websites serve images as WebP because the files are smaller and pages load faster. When you right-click and save a picture, it often lands on your device as .webp. Newer browsers and phones open it, but older photo editors, some printers, and many upload forms still expect JPG or PNG.

How to use the converted files

  • Edit them in any photo app, including older versions of Photoshop and Windows Photos.
  • Upload them to forms and marketplaces that reject WebP.
  • Put them into Word, PowerPoint or a PDF with JPG to PDF.

Only use images you have the right to use; saving a picture from a website doesn't give you permission to reuse it.

Questions

Is WebP better than JPG?

For websites, often yes: WebP files are smaller. For sharing and uploading, JPG is safer because every app and form accepts it.

Do animated WebP images work?

Only the first frame is converted, because JPG can't store animation.

Is quality lost when converting WebP to JPG?

A little, as with any JPG save, but at high quality the difference isn't visible.
