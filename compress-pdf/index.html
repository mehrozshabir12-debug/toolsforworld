Compress PDF

Shrink large PDFs, especially scans and documents full of photos, so they fit email and upload limits.

How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Pick a level: Strong suits most scans, Light keeps more detail.
  3. Press Compress PDF and download the smaller file.

Good to know

Each page is re-saved as a compressed image. That is why scanned PDFs often get 60–90% smaller. Text in the result can no longer be selected or searched. If your PDF is mostly typed text, it is probably already small.

Questions

How much smaller will my PDF get?

Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs usually shrink by 50–90%. Text-only PDFs may not shrink.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit. Very large PDFs (hundreds of pages) can be slow on phones.
