JPG to PDF under 500KB

Turn photos or phone scans into a single PDF that is just under 500 KB, ready for visa, scholarship and bank forms that allow up to 500 KB.

How to make a PDF under 500 KB from photos

  1. Choose your JPG, PNG or WebP photos. Use the arrows to put the pages in order.
  2. The limit is already set to 500 KB. Change it if your form allows a different size.
  3. Press Create PDF and download the file.

How it works

The PDF is first made at full quality. If it is bigger than 500 KB, every photo is re-saved a little smaller and the tool searches for the sharpest level at which the whole PDF still fits. Nothing is uploaded: your photos are processed by your own browser.

How many pages fit in 500 KB?

500 KB leaves room for several pages. Five to ten phone-scanned pages typically fit with clear text. Fewer pages always means sharper pages.

Example

A typical case: a visa application asks for three months of bank statements as one PDF under 500 KB. Photograph each page, choose them all here and press Create PDF. With six to nine pages, 500 KB keeps the figures easy to read.

Tips for 500 KB

  • Remove pages the form does not ask for, such as terms and conditions pages, to keep the rest sharp.
  • Statements with small figures need the most space: check the smallest numbers in the result.
  • Need several separate PDFs? Make each one here, then join them with Merge PDF only if the form wants a single file.
  • Already have a PDF that is too big? Use Compress PDF to 500KB instead.

Questions

Is this the same as “image to PDF under 500 KB” or “photo to PDF less than 500 KB”?

Yes. JPG to PDF, image to PDF and photo to PDF all mean the same here, and under, below, less than and up to 500 KB all mean the PDF must be 500 KB or smaller.

What if the PDF can't get under 500 KB?

The tool shows the smallest size it could reach. Remove a page, crop the photos tighter or use a higher limit if your form allows it.

Will a 10-page document fit under 500 KB?

Often, if the pages are mostly text. Pages with photos or coloured tables may need to be split into two PDFs or reduced to the pages the form actually needs.

Can I do this on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone's browser, choose photos from your gallery and download the PDF. No app is needed.
