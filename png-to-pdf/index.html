PNG to PDF converter

Convert one or many PNG images, screenshots and scans into a single PDF. PNGs go in without being re-compressed, so text and lines stay sharp. Nothing is uploaded.

How to convert PNG to PDF

  1. Choose one or more PNG files (you can add JPG and WebP too).
  2. Put the pages in order with the ▲ ▼ arrows.
  3. Pick a page size (A4, Letter or Same as image) and a margin.
  4. Press Create PDF and download images.pdf.

Why PNG to PDF works well here

PNG is a lossless format, which is why it's the usual format for screenshots, charts, diagrams, receipts saved from apps and scanned forms. This tool puts each PNG into the PDF as it is: the pixels are not re-saved as JPG, so small text, thin lines and flat colours keep their exact edges. Transparent areas show as white on the page, like on paper.

Common uses

  • Screenshots to one document: a series of app screens, chat messages or error messages for a support ticket or a report.
  • Homework and notes: phone scans saved as PNG, combined into one PDF for an online submission.
  • Receipts and invoices: PNG receipts from banking or shopping apps, ready to attach to an expense claim.
  • Design proofs: export artboards as PNG and send one PDF for review.

Page size, margins and file size

  • A4 suits most countries; Letter suits the US and Canada. Wide images get a landscape page automatically.
  • Same as image makes each page exactly the size of its picture, with no white border. Good for screenshots read on screen.
  • PNG files are large compared with JPG. If the PDF must stay under an upload limit, type it in Max PDF size (for example 500 KB). The images are then re-saved as JPG at the best quality that fits, which also turns transparent areas white.

Have photos rather than screenshots? JPG to PDF is the same tool set up for camera photos. Need the opposite? Turn PDF pages into images with PDF to PNG.

Questions

Is PNG to PDF free?

Yes, with no limits, sign-up or watermark.

Will my PNG lose quality in the PDF?

No. Without a size limit, PNGs are embedded losslessly, exactly as they are. Only when you set a Max PDF size are they re-saved as JPG to fit.

Can I combine several PNG files into one PDF?

Yes. Choose them all at once, reorder them with the arrows, and they become one PDF with one image per page.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

Transparent areas appear white on the PDF page.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The PDF is made inside your browser, so the files never leave your device.

Can I convert PNG to PDF on iPhone or Android?

Yes. Open this page in Safari or Chrome, choose the images from Photos or Files, and download the PDF.
