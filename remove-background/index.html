Background remover

Remove the background from a photo automatically and download a transparent PNG.

How to remove a background

  1. Choose a photo.
  2. Press Remove background. The first time, the AI model downloads once.
  3. Download the transparent PNG.

Works best when the subject (a person, product or pet) stands out clearly from the background. Use the PNG for profile pictures, product photos and designs.

Where a transparent background helps

  • Product photos for online shops and marketplaces that want a clean white or plain background.
  • Profile pictures on a coloured circle for LinkedIn, CVs or team pages.
  • Designs: put a person or object onto a poster, thumbnail or card.
  • Stickers for chat apps.

Getting a clean cut-out

The AI separates the main subject from everything else, so it works best with one clear subject, good light and some contrast with the background. Fine hair, glass, smoke and objects the same colour as the wall are the hardest parts; zoom in on the edges before you use the result in print.

The output is a PNG, because JPG can't store transparency. Need a white background instead? Open the PNG and convert it with PNG to JPG, which fills transparent areas with white.

Questions

Is it free with no limits?

Yes. The AI runs on your own device, so there are no credits or limits.

Why is the first run slow?

The AI model downloads the first time (tens of MB). After that, your browser keeps it.

Is my photo sent to an AI server?

No. The AI model runs inside your browser on your own device.

Can I use it for passport photos?

Many passport rules ban edited backgrounds. Check the rules first, or take the photo against a plain light wall.
