Character counter

Paste your text to count characters with and without spaces as you type. Handy for posts, bios, ads and SMS.

Common character limits

  • X (Twitter) post: 280 characters.
  • SMS: 160 characters per message (70 with emoji or non-Latin letters).
  • Instagram bio: 150 characters.
  • Google meta description: about 155 characters before it gets cut off.
  • Google Ads headline: 30 characters; description: 90.

Emoji and many non-Latin letters count as one character here, the way people read them.

Questions

Do spaces count as characters?

Usually yes. That's why both numbers are shown: with spaces and without spaces.

Is my text saved?

No. Counting happens in your browser and nothing is stored.
