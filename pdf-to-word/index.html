PDF to Word converter

Turn the text of a PDF into an editable Word document. Your file is processed on your device and never uploaded.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Press Convert to Word.
  3. Download the .docx file and open it in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

What gets converted

The text of every page, in reading order, with paragraphs and headings. Each PDF page starts on a new page in Word. Pictures, tables, columns and exact fonts are not copied, so check the layout after converting.

A scanned PDF is just pictures of pages, with no real text inside. Use Image to Text (OCR) to read it.

When PDF to Word is the right choice

Use it when you need to edit the words of a document you only have as a PDF: updating an old CV, reusing paragraphs from a report, fixing a typo in a letter, or pulling notes out of lecture slides. You get a .docx file with the text in reading order, which you can restyle in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice Writer.

If you need the PDF to look exactly the same, editing it as Word is the wrong route: fonts, columns and image positions are rebuilt from scratch, not copied. In that case keep the PDF and add notes or a signature in a PDF reader instead.

Checking the result

  • Scroll through quickly and look for words joined together or split across lines. Multi-column layouts are the usual cause.
  • Re-apply headings with Word's built-in styles so the document has a proper outline.
  • Add pictures back by hand from the original file if you need them.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no limit, sign-up or watermark.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs inside your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

Why is my Word file empty?

The PDF is probably a scan. Scans contain images, not text; run them through OCR first.

Can I convert a PDF on my phone?

Yes. It works in the browser on Android and iPhone. Open the .docx in the Word or Google Docs app.

Why are some words in the wrong order?

PDFs store text as positioned fragments. Tables, columns and text boxes can come out in an unexpected order, so check those parts.
