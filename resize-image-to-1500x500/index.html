Resize image to 1500×500 pixels

Get a photo that is exactly 1500 × 500 px for X (Twitter) profile header images. Choose crop to fill, or fit with a white border.

How to resize to 1500×500

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. The size is already set to 1500 × 500.
  3. Pick Crop to fill (no empty space) or Fit with white border (nothing cut off), then press Resize.

Why exact size matters

Platforms crop or blur images that are the wrong shape. Uploading at exactly 1500 × 500 px keeps your photo sharp and framed the way you want.

X (Twitter) header tips

The header on an X profile is 1500 × 500 px, a 3:1 strip.

  • Bottom-left is covered by your round profile picture.
  • Top and bottom are trimmed on some screens, so keep the important part in the middle band.
  • JPG or PNG both work. Use PNG for logos and text so the edges stay crisp.

Uploading a wide photo? Crop to fill gives a full header with no white edges.

Questions

Will my photo be stretched?

No. Crop to fill keeps the proportions and trims the edges; Fit with white border keeps the whole photo and adds a border.

Can I use the same image for LinkedIn and X?

Not exactly: X is 3:1 and LinkedIn is 4:1. Resize the same photo twice, once to 1500×500 and once to 1584×396.
