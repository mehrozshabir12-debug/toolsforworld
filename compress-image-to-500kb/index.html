Compress image to 500KB

Choose a photo and it is made just under 500 KB automatically, keeping as much quality as possible. Ideal for high-quality photos that still upload fast.

How to compress a photo to 500KB

  1. Choose your photo (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. The target is already set to 500 KB. Change it if you need a different size.
  3. Press Compress and download the photo.

How it works

The tool tries many quality levels and picks the highest one that stays under 500 KB. If the photo is still too big at the lowest quality, it is scaled down a little at a time until it fits. Everything happens on your device, so your photo is never uploaded.

Tips

  • Crop away empty background first: less detail means better quality at 500 KB.
  • Most forms want JPG. Keep the output on JPG unless WebP is allowed.

How big is a 500 KB photo?

For a typical phone photo saved as JPG, 500 KB fits roughly about 1400 × 1900 pixels at good quality. Simple images (a signature, a face on a plain wall) fit more pixels; busy scenes with lots of detail fit fewer.

500 KB keeps photos detailed enough to zoom in, while still uploading quickly on mobile data.

Where 500 KB limits appear

500 KB is useful for high-quality photos that still upload quickly: online shop products, portfolios, real-estate listings and photos sent over mobile data. Viewers can still zoom in a little without seeing blur.

Questions

Is this the same as “resize image to 500 KB”?

Yes. When a form asks you to resize a photo to 500 KB, it means the file size. This tool lowers the file size to 500 KB and only reduces the width and height if it has to.

Will my photo still look good at 500KB?

For most photos, yes. The tool keeps the best quality that fits. Very large, detailed photos may be scaled down to fit.

Can I compress several photos to 500KB at once?

Yes. Choose several files and each one is compressed to the target size.

Is 500 KB enough to print a photo?

For small prints such as 4×6 inch, usually yes. For larger prints, keep a bigger original.
