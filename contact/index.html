Contact

Found a bug, or want a new tool? We would love to hear from you.

Email: hello@toolsforworld.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few working days.

Reporting a problem

Tell us which tool you used, your device and browser (for example “iPhone, Safari” or “Windows, Chrome”), and what happened. A screenshot of any error message helps a lot. Please don't send us private documents: our tools never need your files, because everything runs in your browser.

Suggesting a tool

Describe the job you are trying to do, for example “I need to turn a photo into a 35 × 45 mm print with a blue background”. If a form or website asks for a specific size or format, include its exact wording.

Before you write

  • Questions about how your data is handled are answered in our privacy policy.
  • Rules for using the site are in our terms of use.
  • The open-source software we use is listed on the credits page.
