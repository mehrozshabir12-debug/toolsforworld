How to extract text from an image

  1. Choose a photo or screenshot.
  2. Pick the language of the text.
  3. Press Extract text, then copy it or download a .txt file.

Tips for better results

  • Use a sharp, well-lit photo taken straight on.
  • Crop away everything except the text.
  • Printed text works much better than handwriting.

What OCR is good for

OCR (optical character recognition) turns the letters in a picture into text you can copy. People use it to grab text from screenshots, copy quotes from a photographed book page, digitise printed letters and notices, or pull a phone number or address from a photo.

Why results are sometimes wrong

  • Wrong language selected: pick the language the text is written in; this matters most for Urdu, Arabic, Hindi and Chinese.
  • Blurry or tilted photo: retake it closer, with steady hands and good light.
  • Busy background: text on patterns or photos is harder to read than black on white.
  • Stylised fonts and handwriting: decorative fonts and joined-up writing often fail.

Always proofread names, numbers and dates before you use the text. Have a scanned PDF instead of an image? Save the pages as images with PDF to JPG first.

Questions

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian and Simplified Chinese.

Can it read handwriting?

Clear handwriting sometimes works, but OCR is built for printed text.

Is my image uploaded for OCR?

No. The recognition engine runs in your browser, so the image stays on your device.

Why is the first run slower?

The language data downloads the first time you use a language; later runs are faster.
