German image to text (OCR)

Extract German text from a photo or screenshot so you can copy, edit and search it. Works for printed German text, including umlauts, from documents and screenshots.

German from official letters and old documents

A common use is copying text from letters from German authorities, insurers and landlords, so it can be pasted into a translator. Old documents printed in Fraktur (blackletter) type, common before the 1940s, are not read well by this model; it's built for modern Latin type.

Text aus Bildern auf Deutsch erkennen: Foto oder Screenshot wählen und den Text kopieren. Ihr Bild wird nicht hochgeladen.

About German text recognition

The German model reads umlauts (ä ö ü) and ß. It is built for modern printed fonts: old blackletter (Fraktur) books from before about 1940 are not recognised well.

Tips for German documents

  • Long compound words are often hyphenated across lines in letters and contracts. Search the result for “- ” and join the parts.
  • ß and ss: Swiss German uses ss everywhere, so don't “correct” Swiss text.
  • Umlauts can lose their dots in small print (ü → u). Words like “für” and “über” are the usual victims.
  • Capitalised nouns help you spot errors: a noun in lower case is often a misread letter.

Questions

Can I translate the text after OCR?

Yes. Copy the result into any translator; because it's real text, the translation is much better than with a photo.

Can it read old Fraktur (blackletter) text?

Not reliably. This model is trained on modern fonts.
