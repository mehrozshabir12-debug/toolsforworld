Password generator

Create strong random passwords. They are made on your device with a secure random generator and never sent anywhere.

What makes a password strong?

  • Length matters most: use 16 characters or more.
  • Mix upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols.
  • Use a different password for every account, and keep them in a password manager.

Questions

Are these passwords safe to use?

Yes. They are generated on your device with crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographic random generator, and are never sent or saved.
