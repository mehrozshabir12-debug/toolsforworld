Resize image to 1080×1920 pixels

Get a photo that is exactly 1080 × 1920 px for Instagram stories, Reels, TikTok and WhatsApp status. Choose crop to fill, or fit with a white border. Need a different size, like 3000 × 400 or 700 × 500? Just type your own width and height in the boxes.

Quick answer: 1080 × 1920 is the vertical Full HD format: 1080 px wide and 1920 px tall, a 9:16 shape. It is the same as 1920 × 1080 turned on its side, and it fills a phone screen exactly.

How to resize to 1080×1920

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. The size is already set to 1080 × 1920.
  3. Pick Crop to fill (no empty space) or Fit with white border (nothing cut off), then press Resize.

Why exact size matters

Platforms crop or blur images that are the wrong shape. Uploading at exactly 1080 × 1920 px keeps your photo sharp and framed the way you want.

Stories, Reels and TikTok: mind the safe zone

1080 × 1920 px is the full-screen vertical 9:16 shape used by Instagram Stories and Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Stories and WhatsApp status.

  • Top 250 px: covered by the profile name, progress bar and close button.
  • Bottom 350 px: covered by the caption, reply box and music label.
  • Right edge: on Reels and TikTok the like, comment and share buttons sit here.

Put important text and faces in the central area. If your photo is landscape, choose Fit with white border to keep all of it, or Crop to fill and make sure the subject is in the centre.

Questions

Will my photo be stretched?

No. Crop to fill keeps the proportions and trims the edges; Fit with white border keeps the whole photo and adds a border.

Can I resize to a size other than 1080×1920?

Yes. Type any width and height in pixels (up to 20,000) in the size boxes, for example 3000 × 400, 1500 × 400 or 600 × 750, and press Resize.

Why does my story photo look zoomed in?

The photo was not 9:16, so the app cropped it to fill the screen. Resize it to 1080×1920 first and you decide what gets cut.

What is 1080x1920 format?

A vertical 9:16 image or video frame, 1080 pixels wide and 1920 tall. Stories, Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status all use it.

Is 1080x1920 the same as 1920x1080?

Same number of pixels, different direction. 1920 × 1080 is landscape (16:9); 1080 × 1920 is portrait (9:16).
