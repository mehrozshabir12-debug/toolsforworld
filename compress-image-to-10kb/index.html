Compress image to 10KB

Choose a photo and it is made just under 10 KB automatically, keeping as much quality as possible. Ideal for signatures and thumbnail-size form fields.

How to compress a photo to 10KB

  1. Choose your photo (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. The target is already set to 10 KB. Change it if you need a different size.
  3. Press Compress and download the photo.

How it works

The tool tries many quality levels and picks the highest one that stays under 10 KB. If the photo is still too big at the lowest quality, it is scaled down a little at a time until it fits. Everything happens on your device, so your photo is never uploaded.

Tips

  • Crop away empty background first: less detail means better quality at 10 KB.
  • Most forms want JPG. Keep the output on JPG unless WebP is allowed.

How big is a 10 KB photo?

For a typical phone photo saved as JPG, 10 KB fits roughly about 150 × 200 pixels at good quality. Simple images (a signature, a face on a plain wall) fit more pixels; busy scenes with lots of detail fit fewer.

Signatures: sign with a dark pen on plain white paper, photograph it in good light and crop tightly around the signature. At 10 KB, a tightly cropped signature stays sharp.

Where 10 KB limits appear

10 KB is the smallest common limit, and it is mostly used for signature and thumb impression uploads on exam and government forms. A full colour photo cannot look good at 10 KB, so crop tightly around the signature, and use black ink on white paper.

Getting a signature to 10 KB without blur

Signature uploads are judged on whether the strokes are clear, not on colour. Sign a little larger than normal with a dark blue or black pen, photograph it on a flat surface without shadows, and crop so the signature fills almost the whole image. A wide strip of empty paper wastes bytes that the ink needs.

If the result still looks fuzzy, the photo had too many pixels to begin with: resize it to about 300 pixels wide with the Image Resizer, then compress again.

Questions

Is this the same as “resize image to 10 KB”?

Yes. When a form asks you to resize a photo to 10 KB, it means the file size. This tool lowers the file size to 10 KB and only reduces the width and height if it has to.

Will my photo still look good at 10KB?

For most photos, yes. The tool keeps the best quality that fits. Very large, detailed photos may be scaled down to fit.

Can I compress several photos to 10KB at once?

Yes. Choose several files and each one is compressed to the target size.

Can a passport-size photo be 10 KB?

Only at a very small size. Most forms that ask for 10 KB want a signature, not a photo. Check whether the photo field allows 20–50 KB.

What does a 10 KB photo look like in pixels?

A 10 KB JPG is usually around 150 × 200 pixels, enough for a signature or a tiny form photo. The exact size depends on how much detail the picture has.

How do I check my photo's size in KB?

On Windows, right-click the photo and choose Properties. On a Mac, select it and press Cmd + I. On Android or iPhone, open the photo and look at its details or info. The tool also shows the new size after compressing.

Does this work for “below 10 KB”, “less than 10 KB” or “up to 10 KB” limits?

Yes. Under, below, less than and up to 10 KB all mean the photo must be 10 KB or smaller, and the result is made just under 10 KB. If a form gives a range instead, the result lands just under 10 KB, at the top of that range.
