Date calculator

Count the days between two dates, or add and subtract days, weeks, months or business days. Results update as you type.

Days between two dates

Add or subtract from a date

How to count the days between two dates

  1. Pick the start date and the end date.
  2. Read the total days, plus the same span in years, months and days, weeks, weekdays and weekend days.
  3. Tick Include the end date when both days count, for example for a hotel stay counted in days, a leave request or an event that runs from the 1st to the 5th (5 days, not 4).

How to add or subtract days from a date

  1. Pick the start date.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract, type the amount and choose days, weeks, months, years or business days.
  3. The result shows the date and its weekday, for example "30 days from today" or "90 days before a deadline".

How the counting works

  • Days between counts calendar days from the start date up to the end date. The start date counts and the end date doesn't, unless you tick the box. 1 March to 2 March is 1 day.
  • Weekdays are Monday to Friday. Public holidays are not removed, because they differ by country and region.
  • Adding months keeps the same day number. If that day doesn't exist (31 January + 1 month), the result is the last day of the month (28 or 29 February).
  • Business days skip Saturdays and Sundays. Adding 10 business days to a Friday lands on the Friday two weeks later.
  • Leap years are included, and daylight-saving changes don't shift the result by a day.

Everyday examples

  • Deadlines: a contract says payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.
  • Visas and travel: count how many days you'll stay, or the date 90 days after you arrive.
  • Notice periods: find the last working day after four weeks' notice.
  • Pregnancy and baby milestones: count weeks from a date.
  • Countdowns: days left until an exam, a wedding or the end of the year.

Questions

How many days are there between two dates?

Pick both dates above. The count starts on the start date and stops before the end date; tick Include the end date to count both days.

Does it count weekends and holidays?

The total counts every calendar day. It also shows weekdays (Monday to Friday) and weekend days separately. Public holidays are not removed.

What is 30, 60 or 90 days from today?

Use Add or subtract: the start date is today by default, so choose Add, type the number of days and read the date.

How are business days added?

Saturdays and Sundays are skipped. For example, 5 business days after a Monday is the next Monday.

What happens when I add a month to 31 January?

The result is the last day of February (28 or 29), because February has no 31st.

Is anything I type saved?

No. The dates are calculated in your browser and not stored.
