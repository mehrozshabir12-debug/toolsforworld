Compress PDF to 1MB

Choose a PDF and it is made just under 1 MB, keeping as much quality as possible. Ideal for email, WhatsApp and portals with a 1 MB limit.

Quick answer: To make a PDF less than 1 MB: choose the file, keep the target at 1 MB and press Compress PDF. The tool keeps the sharpest version that still fits under 1 MB.

How to compress a PDF to 1MB

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. The target is already set to 1 MB. Change it if your form allows a different size.
  3. Press Compress PDF and download the smaller file.

How it works

Each page is re-saved as a compressed image. The tool tries several quality levels and keeps the sharpest one that still fits under 1 MB. Your file never leaves your device.

Tips

  • Scanned documents and PDFs full of photos shrink the most.
  • Fewer pages means better quality at 1 MB. Remove pages you don't need with Split PDF first.
  • Text in the result can no longer be selected or searched, which is fine for forms and scans.

What fits in 1 MB?

1 MB is the limit on many university and job portals. It holds roughly ten to twenty scanned pages at good quality, or a short report with photos.

  • Application packs: merge your certificates, ID and letters into one file, then compress to 1 MB.
  • Photos inside: PDFs full of photos shrink the most.
  • Sharing on WhatsApp or email: a 1 MB PDF opens quickly even on slow mobile data.

Questions

What if my PDF can't get under 1MB?

The tool shows the smallest size it could reach. Split the PDF into parts or remove pages, then try again.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Compression happens inside your browser, so the file stays on your device.

My PDF is 1.2 MB. Will compressing to 1 MB hurt quality?

Barely. The tool only lowers quality as much as needed, so a small cut like this is usually invisible.

How do I check if my PDF is under 1 MB?

On Windows, right-click the file and choose Properties. On a Mac, select it and press Cmd + I. On a phone, open the file's details in your Files app. 1 MB is 1,024 KB.
