Image resolutions explained: 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K and square sizes

By Mehroz Shabir · Updated 16 October 2026

Forms, platforms and screens ask for sizes like 1920×1080 or 3000×3000. Here is what those numbers mean, which shape each one is, and how to get your photo to exactly the right size.

Reading a size like 1920 × 1080

The first number is the width in pixels, the second is the height. So 1920 × 1080 is a wide landscape image and 1080 × 1920 is the same image turned upright. Multiply the two numbers to get the total pixels: 1920 × 1080 = 2,073,600 pixels, about 2.1 megapixels.

Pixels and print size

A printed size depends on the dpi (dots per inch). Divide the pixels by the dpi to get inches: 600 px at 300 dpi prints at 2 inches, and 3000 px at 300 dpi prints at 10 inches. Online forms only care about the pixels; printers care about the dpi too.

Pixels are not KB

Width and height in pixels is the dimension. KB or MB is the file size, which also depends on how much detail the photo has and how strongly it is compressed. Two 1080 × 1080 photos can be 150 KB and 900 KB. If a form says “under 100 KB”, use Compress Image to 100 KB instead of resizing.

Changing the shape: crop or border

When your photo is a different shape from the size you need, something has to give. Crop to fill trims the edges so the frame is full; Fit with white border keeps the whole photo and adds space. Our image resizer lets you choose, and works on phones without uploading your photo.

Enlarging a small photo to a big size such as 3000 × 3000 gives the right dimensions, but it can't add detail that was never there. Start from the largest original you have.

Questions

What is the difference between 1080p and 1080x1920?

1080p usually means 1920 × 1080 landscape. 1080 × 1920 is the same number of pixels in a vertical 9:16 shape, used for phone-screen content like Stories and Reels.

Is 2560x1440 2K or 1440p?

Both names are used. 2560 × 1440 is officially QHD (Quad HD), commonly called 1440p, and many monitors sell it as 2K.

How many megapixels is 3000x3000?

9 megapixels: 3000 × 3000 = 9,000,000 pixels.

How do I resize a photo to an exact pixel size for free?
