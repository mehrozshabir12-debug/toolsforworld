Chinese image to text (OCR)

Extract Chinese text from a photo or screenshot so you can copy, edit and search it. Works for printed Simplified Chinese text from documents and screenshots.

How to convert Chinese image to text

  1. Choose a clear photo or screenshot.
  2. The language is already set to Chinese.
  3. Press Extract text and copy the result.

Tips for accurate Chinese OCR

  • Use a sharp image with dark text on a light background.
  • Crop to the text only, and keep lines straight.
  • Printed text works much better than handwriting.

About Chinese text recognition

This model reads Simplified Chinese (mainland China, Singapore). Traditional characters used in Taiwan and Hong Kong are less accurate. Horizontal text works best; text printed in vertical columns may come out in the wrong order.

What people use Chinese OCR for

Copies Simplified Chinese text from documents, product labels, signs and screenshots of chat apps, ready to paste into a translator.

After extracting, paste the text into Word or Google Docs to fix any mistakes. For multi-page product labels and screenshots, turn each page into an image with PDF to JPG first, then extract them one by one.

Questions

Is Chinese OCR free?

Yes, with no limits. The text recognition runs on your own device.

Why are some words wrong?

Blurry photos, unusual fonts and handwriting reduce accuracy. Try a clearer, cropped image.

Can it read Traditional Chinese?

Some characters are shared, but accuracy is lower. This page uses the Simplified Chinese model.

Can I extract Chinese text from a PDF?

Yes. Convert the PDF pages to images with PDF to JPG, then run each image through Chinese OCR. If the PDF already has selectable text, PDF to Word is faster.
