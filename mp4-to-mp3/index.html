MP4 to MP3 converter

Extract the audio from a video file and save it as MP3. The file stays on your device.

How to convert MP4 to MP3

  1. Choose a video (MP4, MOV, WebM).
  2. Pick the quality: 192 kbps suits most music and speech.
  3. Press Convert to MP3.
Only convert videos you own or have permission to use. Downloading from YouTube is not supported.

Which quality should I pick?

  • 128 kbps: speech, lectures and podcasts. About 1 MB per minute.
  • 192 kbps: music and general use. About 1.4 MB per minute.
  • 320 kbps: the best MP3 quality. About 2.4 MB per minute.

Supported files

MP4, MOV, M4A and WebM work in most browsers. MKV and other formats work when your browser can play their audio. While converting, each minute of audio needs roughly 30–40 MB of memory, so recordings longer than about an hour can fail on phones; use a computer for those.

Questions

Is there a size limit?

No fixed limit, but very long videos need a lot of memory and can fail on phones. Use a computer for videos over about 200 MB.

Can I convert a YouTube link?

No. This tool converts files that are already on your device. Only convert videos you own or have permission to use.
