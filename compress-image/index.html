Image compressor

Reduce photo file size for online forms, email and websites. Lower quality gives a smaller file.

How to compress an image

  1. Choose one or more photos.
  2. Set the quality (70% keeps photos sharp for most uses).
  3. Press Compress and save each smaller file.

Need a photo under 100 KB or 50 KB for an application form? Lower the quality and set a maximum width such as 1200 px.

Questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Compression happens inside your browser.
